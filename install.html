Get the RLL Discipleship app

LivingSeed · Revival Labors Liberia — Duazon/Boystown Field

You can put the discipleship tracker on your phone like any other app — no Play Store or App Store needed. It takes less than 1 MB, opens quickly, and has its own icon called RLL Disciples.

You need: internet (to sign in and save) and the access PIN your administrator gave you.

Open the app
Android phoneiPhone / iPadComputerUpdatingProblems

Android phone (Chrome)

  1. Open the app link in Chrome.
  2. On the sign-in screen, tap 📲 Install the app.
    No button? Tap the menu ⋮ at the top right and choose Install app or Add to Home screen.
  3. Tap Install.
  4. Find the RLL Disciples icon on your home screen. Open it and sign in with your PIN.
Opened the link inside WhatsApp? WhatsApp's own browser cannot install apps. Tap ⋮ and choose Open in Chrome first.

iPhone or iPad (Safari)

  1. Open the app link in Safari.
    From WhatsApp or another app: tap ⋯ or the compass icon → Open in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button — the square with an arrow pointing up (at the bottom of the screen, or the top on iPad).
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Tap Add. Open RLL Disciples from your home screen and sign in.

Computer (Chrome or Edge)

  1. Open the app link.
  2. Click the install icon at the right end of the address bar (a screen with a down arrow), or the menu ⋮ → Install RLL Disciples.
  3. Click Install. The app opens in its own window and is added to your Start menu or Applications.

Keeping the app up to date

When we improve the app, you will see this bar at the bottom of the screen:

A new version of the app is ready.   Update now Later

  1. Save any attendance or changes you are working on.
  2. Tap Update now. The app reloads in a few seconds and you stay signed in.

Tapped Later? The bar comes back the next time you open the app. To check for an update yourself, close the app completely and open it again. Your version number is shown under the Sign in button.

If something is not working

“Could not reach the tracker”
Check your data or Wi-Fi and try again. Marks you made are kept on your phone until they are saved.
I don't see “Install the app”
The app may already be installed — look for the RLL Disciples icon. Otherwise use the browser menu ⋮ → Install app / Add to Home screen (Android), or Share → Add to Home Screen (iPhone).
It asks me to sign in again
For safety you are signed out after a while. Enter your PIN again.
Wrong PIN or forgot my PIN
Ask your Center Leader or administrator. After several wrong tries you must wait 10 minutes.
Reporting mobilization with no internet
Install the app and sign in once with internet. After that, Mobilization works offline: reports and church locations wait on the phone (📤) and are sent by themselves when you are online. Don't sign out or clear the browser while reports are waiting.
Removing the app
Press and hold the icon → Uninstall or Remove. Nothing in the tracker is deleted, and your other apps are not affected.
Keep it safe: never share your PIN, and tap Sign out when you use someone else's phone.
