How to install and update the app

Help — how to use the app

Getting started

  1. Sign in with the access PIN your administrator gave you. Never share your PIN.
  2. Find your way. On a phone, your main screens are the big buttons at the bottom of the screen. Tap More (bottom right) for every other screen, Help and Sign out. On a computer the screens are listed on the left.
  3. You only see what you are responsible for. A class leader sees their class, a registrar sees the meetings given to them, and so on. Your name and role are shown at the top.
  4. Sign out when you use someone else's phone: on a phone, tap your name at the top right, then Sign out. On a computer, Sign out is under your name on the left.
No network? Marks you make are kept on your phone. When the network comes back, open the same screen and tap Save.
Simple view. Forms show the main details first — tap + More details (optional) for the rest. On a phone, searches show just the search box: tap ⚙ Filters to choose more, and the filters you chose stay visible as small chips (tap ✕ on one to remove it). To have every option open on every screen, tap your name (top right on a phone) → Show more options.
“A new version of the app is ready” — save your work, then tap Update now. You stay signed in.

Disciples (home)

The app opens on Disciples: the people becoming disciples, what each one is doing now, and how to reach them.

  • Needs your attention — only what someone should act on now (decisions waiting, hand-offs, classes not marked, newcomers to invite, follow-ups due). Tap ✓ Done when it is dealt with.
  • Four numbers — attendance rate, people who came, new people, decisions — with ▲ ▼ against the period before.
  • Flow into discipleship classes — for the chosen period: people who came to meetings and outreaches → made a decision → reached by a counselor → joined a class → attending class now. Under it, each class: members, how many joined in the period, how many are attending now. Tap a class to see its disciples.
  • Disciples now — every active disciple with what they are doing: Attending (came to class in the last 2 weeks), Missing class (2–5 weeks), Absent (longer, or never came since joining) or Not in a class. Each shows their class, when they last came, their area, counselor and last contact, a suggested next step, and 📞 call · 💬 SMS · WhatsApp. Tap the buttons at the top to show one group; search, or filter by class or area. Tap a name to open their page.
  • Leaders see only the disciples in what they lead; counselors see theirs.

Statistics and insights

  • Statistics opens with Insights: What the numbers say (plain sentences — where most people and disciples are, areas with many people but no class, classes whose members come from elsewhere, new disciples this month, how many of those mobilized came, which churches sent people), a table of people, disciples, classes and churches by area, where class members live, mobilization: who came, and a map of the churches.
  • Then attendance by week, each class and program, and all the other charts and tables — every table can be downloaded.
  • Set each class's Area (Users & programs → Programs) so classes and areas can be compared.

Meetings and outreaches

Open Meetings. First you see Meetings at a glance: today's meetings, the last 90 days, new faces, decisions waiting, newcomers to invite to a class and regulars who stopped coming.

Mark who came

  1. Tap Mark now on today's meeting, or tap a meeting in the list. Program leaders and center leaders: tap + New meeting to create one first.
  2. Someone who came before: type part of their name or phone number in Find a returning person and tap their name.
  3. Someone new: tap + Register a newcomer, fill in what you can (first and last name are needed) and save. They are marked present.
  4. For people on the list, tap Present, Late or Excused. Tapped by mistake? Tap the same button again to undo.
  5. Tap Save attendance.
Not marked = absent. There is no Absent button for meetings: anyone not marked Present, Late or Excused counts as absent.
  • Search this list and the All / Present / Absent / ✝ Decision buttons help you find people in a long list.
  • The boxes at the top count who registered, who came and the decisions — for this meeting only.
  • ⬇ Contact list downloads everyone on this meeting's list.
  • Women only / Men only meetings only accept people of that gender.

Recording a decision

  1. At the meeting, find the person (mark them present first).
  2. Tap ✝ Decision on their row.
  3. Tick what they decided: To be born again, To rededicate my life to Christ, To follow Jesus in discipleship, Responded to the Word of God, or Other (type what).
  4. Add a short note if you like, and save.

The decision goes to Follow-ups, where a center leader gives the person a counselor.

Discipleship class attendance

Open Class attendance. First you see Your classes at a glance: classes not marked yet, attendance, who missed 3 classes or more, and visitors you can invite.

Mark a class

  1. Tap Mark now, or tap the class. Check the date (it chooses the last class day for you).
  2. Tap Present, Late, Absent or Excused for each person. Mark the rest present does everyone not yet marked.
  3. Tap Save attendance. Anyone on the class list who is not marked is saved as Absent.

New people and visitors

  • Type a name in Add someone to this class. Tap + Add to class to put them on the class list, or Visitor today if they are only visiting.
  • Someone not in the tracker yet: tap + New person. Tick Just visiting today if they are not joining the class.
  • Visitors are marked Visitor for that day only and are never counted absent. If a visitor decides to join, tap + Join class on their row.

When there was no class

Tap No class this day, choose Venue not available, Leader not available or Other reason, and save. Nobody is marked absent for that day. If the class did meet after all, just mark attendance and save.

See all the marks

Switch to Attendance register at the top: everyone against every date, with P, L, A, E, V (visitor) and — (no class). Tap a date to open and change it. ⬇ Download register saves it for Excel.

Assignments

  1. Open Assignments and choose the class.
  2. Tap + New, give the assignment a title and due date, and save.
  3. Open the assignment and tick who completed it. Save.

Sending an SMS to many people

Open Messages. The SMS goes from your own phone with your own credit; the app helps you choose the people, cleans the numbers to +231 … and keeps a record. There are three steps: ① Choose people → ② Write → ③ Send.

① Choose people

  • Quick picks tick a whole group in one tap: a class, who came to the last meeting, decisions in the last 30 days, who missed 3+ classes, new people, or your own saved lists. Tap again to untick.
  • Find people: choose a class or program, then All dates or one date, then Who — everyone on it, who came, or who did not come. Narrow further by gender, area, age group or decision. Then tap Tick all shown, or tick people one by one.
  • Ticks are kept while you change the filters, so you can add people from several programs. Show only ticked lets you check your list.
  • Paste phone numbers from WhatsApp or Excel; they are matched to people where possible.
  • ★ Save ticked people as a list (for example “Choir”) to use it again with one tap.
Shortcut: on Class attendance, Meetings, People and the overview lists, tap ✉ Message — Messages opens with those people already ticked.

② Write

Type the message or start from a template. Long messages are fine — they arrive as one message — but the counter shows how many SMS each costs (160 letters each; emojis and curly quotes make it 70). {first name} works only when sending One by one.

③ Send

  1. Check the numbers that will receive it and who is left out (no valid number, no consent).
  2. Choose Group batches (Android, up to 100 numbers at a time) or One by one (iPhone, or personal messages).
  3. Tap Prepare to send, then Open Messages. Tap Send in Messages, come back, and tap ✓ Sent.
Group batches need one phone setting first so nobody sees the other numbers. Google Messages: profile picture → Messages settings → Advanced → Group messaging → Mass text. Samsung Messages: ⋮ → Settings → More settings → Multimedia messages → Group conversations off.
  • If the app closes while Messages is open, open Messages again — sending carries on where you stopped.
  • A batch that didn't go (no credit, no network): tap ✗ Didn't send, then Open Messages again.
  • To see which messages arrived, turn on Get SMS delivery reports in your Messages settings.

Follow-ups

Open Follow-ups. People are grouped so you know who to contact first: Decision follow-up, Absence follow-up, New participant, Pastoral care and Other. Counselors see the people given to them.

  1. Tap a person (or search a name or phone).
  2. Contact them straight from the app: 📞 calls, 💬 SMS opens a text message, WhatsApp opens a chat. The same buttons are on the dashboard list, decisions and class lists. When you come back, the app offers Log follow-up with how you contacted them already filled in.
  3. Under Log a follow-up, choose how you reached them, write what happened, and the next step if there is one.
  4. For a decision, set its progress: Contacted, Joined a class, Not reachable or Closed. Save.
Center leaders: give each decision a counselor. Women counsel women and men counsel men — the app only offers counselors of the right gender.

Counselor hand-off (center leaders)

  1. Open Follow-ups → Counselor hand-off. You see one counselor at a time, with the people given to them: Not sent yet, Sent — waiting, or Reached ✓. Flip to the next counselor with ‹ ›, by swiping left or right, or by choosing a name from the list (it shows how many are left to send).
  2. Tick the people and tap WhatsApp (full details) — decision, phone, where they live, gender, age, marital status, work and church — or SMS (short) for counselors without a smartphone. If the counselor has no phone number saved, type it in Send to number (Orange 077…/076…, Lonestar 088…/055…); the app remembers it on this phone. Better: add it on Users & programs. Check the text, tap Open WhatsApp / Open SMS, send it, come back and tap ✓ Yes, record it. Told them by phone or in person? Use those buttons instead.
  3. Wrong counselor? Tap Change counselor next to the person. Choose another counselor (same gender only) and tap Move, or tap ✗ Remove counselor (assigned by mistake). The hand-off starts again and a note is kept in Follow-ups.
  4. When the counselor tells you they spoke with someone, tap ✓ Reached (add a short note if you like) — or ✗ Not reachable. The decision moves on and the history is kept in Follow-ups.

Counselors do not need the app. If you change someone's counselor, the hand-off starts again for the new counselor.

People

Open People. The right side first shows a summary of the list (gender, age, area, class). Tap a name to see that person's details; ← Back to the list returns.

  • Search by name, ID, phone or Peace House code. Choose Everyone / Disciples / Participants and All / Active / Inactive.
  • ⚙ Filters narrows the list further: class or program, area, gender, age group, counselor, date joined, attendance, last attended, decision, or a meeting they came to. Each filter shows as a chip; tap it to remove.
  • ⬇ Download saves exactly the people showing: Contact list, Full details (only if allowed), or Choose columns. Phone numbers are only included for people who gave consent.
  • Center leaders and counselors: open a person and tap Edit to correct their details. On the People form you can only put someone in a discipleship class; outreaches are recorded by marking meetings.

Running the tracker

  • Users & programs — add users with their role, PIN and gender (needed for counselors). Tick Can download full details only for people who need it. For each program set who leads it, who marks it, and Who can attend (Everyone, Women only, Men only).
  • Modules — open a user under Users to see their ticks: Disciples (home), Class attendance, Meetings, People, Follow-ups, Counselor hand-off, Assignments, Messages, Statistics, Admin tools. The role gives the usual ticks; tick or untick to change them for that person only. Use the usual modules for the role puts them back. Ticks decide which screens someone gets — they still only see the classes, meetings and people they are given.
  • App settings (a tab in Users & programs) — switch a whole module off for everyone, e.g. Assignments. Nothing is deleted; switching it on brings it back.
  • Areas — Users & programs → App settings: the fixed list of areas (rename, reorder, add). Every person and church picks an area and types the community. Sort old addresses into areas suggests an area for each address typed before; check them and tap Apply. The old text is kept as “Old address”.
  • Mobilizer role — for people who only report mobilization and map churches. They see nothing else.
  • 📤 Share sign-in details — on a user's page under Users. The app makes a new PIN (the old one stops working — for safety the app never shows a saved PIN) and writes a message with the app link, how to install it, the PIN and the help guide for their role. Send it by WhatsApp or SMS to their number, or Copy it. When you add a new user, this opens straight away with the PIN you typed. The Activity log notes that it was sent, never the PIN.
  • Import list — bring in an Excel or CSV list. The app shows who already exists, who might be the same person and who is new before anything is saved. You can undo an import.
  • Import review — check possible duplicates and merge them, or fix missing details in one tap.
  • Activity log — every change, with who made it. Deleted records can be restored from here.
  • In People, ⚙ Filters → Counselor → Counselor of the other gender finds old assignments to correct.

Today

If you do not have the dashboard, the app opens on Today: one big button for each thing you do — the class or meeting to mark, your follow-ups, registering a person, mobilization. Tap a button to go straight there.

Mobilization

Open Mobilize. At the top you see the outreach you are mobilizing for, how many people and churches the team has brought, and how many are yours.

  1. 👤 Report people: type each person's name and phone (tap + Add another for more), then Save people. The same phone number is not counted twice.
  2. ⛪ Report a church: type its name and choose it (or tap + It is not in the list). Tick ✉ Invitation letter delivered, type who received it and their phone (so they can be called to follow up). If the church is missing details, the form for them is right there — standing at the church, tap 📍 Mark the location and take a photo. If everything is already on file, you only see a summary; tap ✏️ Update the church's details or ↻ Update the location / photo when something changed (e.g. a new building). Then Save church report: the letter and the church's details are saved together.
  3. My list: everyone you brought, with 📞 call, 💬 SMS and WhatsApp. ✉ Message my list sends one SMS to all of them from your own phone.
  4. Team: how many each person on the team has brought, and how many of them came (counts only). On My list, people who came show ✓ came.

Mapping churches

  1. Open Map churches. Given to you lists the churches your leader asked you to map. You can also complete any other church.
  2. Tap a church. Each one shows what is missing (location, photo, pastor…).
  3. Stand at the church and tap 📍 Mark the location. Wait until the accuracy is good (±20 m or better), then tap ✓ Use this location. GPS works without internet.
  4. Tap 📷 Take a photo of the building. It is made smaller on your phone so it costs little data.
  5. Fill in what is missing and tap Save. Details already filled in are changed by your leaders only.
No internet? In the installed app you can still report and map. Everything waits on your phone (📤 waiting to send) and is sent by itself when you are online — or tap Send now. If the app asks for your PIN when you are back online, enter it and the reports go. Don't sign out or clear the browser while reports are waiting. On iPhone, install the app to the home screen so nothing is lost.
How “came” works: when a registrar registers someone at the outreach, the app matches their phone number to the people mobilized, and records Their church (chosen on the newcomer form). Nothing has to be typed twice.
📊 Who came (leaders): for each outreach — how many came, how many of the mobilized came (by mobilizer), and how many came from each church, with the churches that got a letter but sent nobody yet.
Leaders: open Outreaches to open an outreach for reports (and close it when mobilization ends). All reports lists every report, by who reported it — tap ✓ Check, or remove a wrong one. Give mapping work under Churches → 👥 Give mapping work: whole areas, or single churches.

Churches

  • Open Churches: every church with its area, what is missing, and how many of your people attend. Tap an area to show only its churches; use the filters for No location, No photo, denomination or who maps it.
  • 🗺 Map shows the mapped churches, coloured by area. On a church, 🧭 Directions opens Google Maps from where you are.
  • ⬆ Import churches reads your Excel file (every sheet). A sheet named after an area puts its churches in that area; otherwise the area comes from the address. Churches already in the list are completed, never doubled.
  • 🔗 Link people to churches groups the church names typed on people's records (all spellings together). Choose the church each one means — or add it as a new church — and everyone in the group is linked. After that, each church shows the people who attend, and Messages can choose people by church.
  • On a person's page, choose their Church from the list.

Good to know

  • Classes: not marked on a class day = absent. Meetings: not marked present = absent.
  • Visitors are never counted absent and are not added to the class list unless they join.
  • No class this day keeps everyone's attendance fair when a class could not meet.
  • Women only / Men only meetings accept only that gender. Women counsel women, men counsel men.
  • Phone numbers are shared in downloads only when the person gave consent.
  • Registering or marking someone does not make you their counselor — a center leader assigns counselors.

If something is not working

“Could not reach the tracker”
Check your data or Wi-Fi and try again. Your marks are kept on the phone until saved.
“Your session has ended”
For safety you are signed out after a while. Sign in again with your PIN.
I can't see a class, meeting or screen
You only see what you are assigned to. Ask your center leader to add you as leader, marker or registrar.
“… is for women only / men only”
That person's gender doesn't match the meeting. Check their gender is recorded correctly.
I made a mistake
Open the date or person again, correct it and save. A center leader can restore deleted records from the Activity log.
Wrong PIN or forgot my PIN
Ask your center leader or administrator. After many wrong tries everyone must wait 10 minutes.
